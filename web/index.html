How the numbers are produced

Daily sales are rolled into ISO weeks. A weighted least-squares line is fitted through the weekly unit history with exponentially decaying weights, giving a current demand level and a trend in units per week. Future weeks extrapolate that trend with damping, so a steep recent slope does not compound indefinitely.

Forecast error is the weighted RMS of the fit residuals. Safety stock is z × σ × √(lead time + review period), where z comes from your service level. A week-by-week simulation then depletes stock by forecast demand, receives orders after the lead time, and orders back up to the target level — those orders, priced at unit cost, are the spend projection.

Every SKU's row shows which basis it used. A SKU with under three weeks of history gets a flat weighted average with no trend, because a slope fitted to two points is noise.
